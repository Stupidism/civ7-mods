[h1]No More Turn（不再来一回合） — 1.8.6[/h1]
A stopping point for "one more turn". Configurable breaks for Civilization VII single-player. English and Simplified Chinese follow the game's text language.

[h2]1. Timed breaks: a reverse Pomodoro[/h2]
Late-game turns can take a while. Set a real stopping point: by default, play for 25 minutes and take a five-minute break. A timed break can begin during a turn; resume when the countdown finishes.

[h2]2. Turn-based breaks[/h2]
Early turns and late turns take different amounts of time. Version 1.8.6 uses reverse Fibonacci defaults: 13 / 8 / 5 / 3 / 2 end-turn actions for turns 1–20 / 21–40 / 41–60 / 61–80 / 81 onward. Existing settings are preserved; use Reset to apply the new defaults. Each interval is adjustable from 1 to 100.
The counter stays in memory: loading does not add turns or reset the count; exiting clears it. Completing a full break resets both time and turn counters.

[h2]3. Voluntary breaks[/h2]
After one idle minute, a voluntary-break panel shows the time already spent away. Continue Game is always available. Five continuous idle minutes complete a break; resuming early keeps your previous play progress.

[h2]4. Restricted hours: early to bed, early to rise[/h2]
The night restriction is enabled by default from 22:00 to 07:00 in your computer's local time. Adjust the schedule in 15-minute steps or disable it independently. Equal start and end times mean no restriction. Exit Game and Esc remain available, and the native exit confirmation appears above the break panel. The end of the restricted period never automatically advances a turn.

[h2]5. Autosave before a break[/h2]
Each break attempts a separate local checkpoint named RestGuard_Idle/Time/Turn/Night with the turn, timestamp and unique suffix. Check the panel's save status before exiting.
Keep the newest 10 break saves by default, adjustable from 1 to 100. Older break saves are removed only after a new save succeeds. The limit spans campaigns. Normal autosaves, quicksaves, other manual saves, cloud saves and other mods' saves are not part of that cleanup. Keep separate saves for important milestones.

[h2]Setup and scope[/h2]
Subscribe, wait for Steam to finish downloading, restart the game, and enable No More Turn in Additional Content. Avoid duplicate manual and Workshop copies. Options → Add-ons → No More Turn contains the settings. Turn, time and night restrictions have independent switches. Turning one off hides its related controls; shared settings remain available. Confirm saves changes, Cancel discards them.
Single-player only. No dependency on another break/save mod. Compatible with Auto-save Before...'s before-turn save hook. The restriction applies inside the game, not to the computer.
1.8.6 passed 78 offline checks against the actual release package. No live game test was run for this defaults-only update. The 1.8.5 break scenarios were captured on Mac 1.5.0 Metal; this does not establish other-platform or physical-controller coverage.

[h2]中文说明[/h2]
给“再来一回合”设个终点。支持《文明 VII》单人游戏，界面跟随游戏文字语言，提供英文和简体中文。

[b]1. 按时间休息，反向番茄钟[/b]
后期两回合可能很久。默认连续游戏25分钟，休息5分钟；不必等当前回合做完，倒计时结束后继续。

[b]2. 按回合休息[/b]
1.8.6改用反向斐波那契默认间隔：第1–20／21–40／41–60／61–80／81回合以后，分别累计13／8／5／3／2次结束回合操作后休息。各档可设1–100。已有配置保留，恢复默认后应用新值。在内存中累计，读档不增加也不清零，退出游戏清零；完整休息共同重置回合与时间计数。

[b]3. 主动休息[/b]
空闲1分钟出现面板，把已经离开的时间计入休息；随时可以继续游戏。连续空闲5分钟完成休息，提前继续则保留之前的累计游戏进度。

[b]4. 禁玩时间，早睡早起[/b]
默认开启22:00–次日07:00的禁玩时间，按电脑本地时间判断。可单独关闭，起止时间按15分钟调整；起止相同不限制。强制休息期间退出游戏和Esc仍可用，退出确认框在上层；解锁后不会自动结束回合。可回主菜单修改设置。

[b]5. 休息前自动存档[/b]
每次休息尝试另存独立RestGuard文件，不覆盖原版自动保存。默认保留最近10个，可设1–100；新存档确认成功后才清理旧休息存档，跨战役合计。原版自动、快速、其他手动、云端与其他Mod存档不参与清理。退出前确认面板显示保存成功，重要进度另外手动保存。

[b]安装与设置[/b]
订阅并等待下载完成，重启游戏，在附加内容启用。避免手动版与工坊版重复安装。设置入口：选项 → 附加内容 → No More Turn（不再来一回合）。回合、时间、禁玩时间可分别开关；关闭时隐藏对应选项，共用选项不受影响。确认保存，取消放弃修改。
