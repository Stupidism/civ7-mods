[h1]No More Turn（不再来一回合） — 1.8.8[/h1]
A stopping point for "one more turn". Configurable breaks for Civilization VII single-player. English and Simplified Chinese follow the game's text language.

[h2]1. Timed breaks: a reverse Pomodoro[/h2]
Set a real stopping point: play for 25 minutes, then take a five-minute break. A timed break can begin during a turn; resume when the countdown finishes.

[h2]2. Turn-based breaks[/h2]
Version 1.8.6 uses reverse Fibonacci defaults: 13 / 8 / 5 / 3 / 2 end-turn actions for turns 1–20 / 21–40 / 41–60 / 61–80 / 81 onward. Existing settings are preserved; use Reset to apply the new defaults. Each interval is adjustable from 1 to 100.
The counter stays in memory: loading does not add turns or reset the count; exiting clears it. Completing a full break resets both time and turn counters.

[h2]3. Voluntary breaks[/h2]
After one idle minute, a voluntary-break panel shows the time already spent away. Continue Game is always available. Five continuous idle minutes complete a break; resuming early keeps your previous play progress.

[h2]4. Restricted hours: early to bed, early to rise[/h2]
The night restriction is enabled by default from 22:00 to 07:00 in your computer's local time. Adjust the schedule in 15-minute steps or disable it independently. Equal start and end times mean no restriction. Use Open settings for the native pause menu, or Esc for native exit confirmation. The end of the restricted period never automatically advances a turn.

[h2]5. Manual saving during breaks[/h2]
Open settings on the break panel opens the native pause menu, where you can save, quick-save or change options yourself. Closing the menu returns to the break panel; the countdown continues and a required break cannot be skipped. This mod never creates, queries or deletes saves in any break mode; existing saves are not moved or deleted. Auto-save Before... keeps handling its own before-turn saves.

[h2]Setup and scope[/h2]
Subscribe, wait for Steam to finish downloading, restart the game, and enable No More Turn in Additional Content. Avoid duplicate manual and Workshop copies. Options → Add-ons → No More Turn contains the settings. Turn, time and night restrictions have independent switches. Turning one off hides its related controls; shared settings remain available. Confirm saves changes, Cancel discards them.
Single-player only. No dependency on another break/save mod. Compatible with Auto-save Before...'s before-turn save hook. The restriction applies inside the game, not to the computer.
The runtime in 1.8.8 passed user acceptance on 2026-11-06. The gallery shots come from 1.8.5 on Mac 1.5.0 Metal; other platforms and physical controllers are not covered.

[b]1. 按时间休息，反向番茄钟[/b]
后期两回合可能很久。默认连续游戏25分钟，休息5分钟；不必等当前回合做完，倒计时结束后继续。

[b]2. 按回合休息[/b]
1.8.6改用反向斐波那契默认间隔：第1–20／21–40／41–60／61–80／81回合以后，分别累计13／8／5／3／2次结束回合操作后休息。各档可设1–100。已有配置保留，恢复默认后应用新值。在内存中累计，读档不增加也不清零，退出游戏清零；完整休息共同重置回合与时间计数。

[b]3. 主动休息[/b]
空闲1分钟出现面板，把已经离开的时间计入休息；随时可以继续游戏。连续空闲5分钟完成休息，提前继续则保留之前的累计游戏进度。

[b]4. 禁玩时间，早睡早起[/b]
默认开启22:00–次日07:00的禁玩时间，按电脑本地时间判断。可单独关闭，起止时间按15分钟调整；起止相同不限制。休息期间可通过“打开设置”进入原版菜单修改设置或退出，也可按Esc退出；解锁后不会自动结束回合。

[b]5. 休息期间手动保存[/b]
休息面板的“打开设置”进入原版暂停菜单，可手动保存、快速保存或调整选项。关闭菜单回到休息面板，倒计时继续，强制休息不能提前跳过。本 Mod 在任何休息模式下都不创建、查询或删除存档；已有存档不搬迁、不删除。Auto-save Before... 的回合前存档仍由该 Mod 自己负责。

[b]安装与设置[/b]
订阅并等待下载完成，重启游戏，在附加内容启用。避免手动版与工坊版重复安装。设置入口：选项 → 附加内容 → No More Turn（不再来一回合）。回合、时间、禁玩时间可分别开关；关闭时隐藏对应选项，共用选项不受影响。确认保存，取消放弃修改。
