[h1]No More Turn（不再来一回合） — 1.8.5[/h1]
Renamed from Rest Guard in 1.8.3; existing subscriptions and settings carry over.
1.8.2 fixes Chinese text appearing as boxes by using the game’s language-aware font style.

Configurable breaks for Civilization VII single-player. The interface follows the game's text language: English and Simplified Chinese. The mod name remains bilingual.

[h2]Breaks and settings[/h2]
1.8.5: Turning a switch off hides its related controls immediately. Turning it back on restores their values. Shared break duration, save retention and reset stay available. Confirm saves changes; Cancel discards them.
1.8.4: Each switch is followed by its related settings. Subtle thin dividers separate sections; shared break duration and save retention appear afterward.
Open Options → Additional Content → No More Turn（不再来一回合）. Confirm Changes saves your settings. Turn, time, and night restrictions have independent switches.
[list]
[*]Turn breaks count end-turn actions in memory. Loading preserves the count without adding turns; exiting clears it. Default intervals are 5/4/3/2/1 across 20-turn stages, adjustable to 1–100.
[*]Timed breaks: play 25 minutes, then rest 5 minutes by default. Both durations are adjustable.
[*]Voluntary breaks: after one idle minute, show a break panel with an always-available Continue Game button. Five idle minutes complete a break and reset both counters; continuing early preserves your progress.
[*]Night restriction: enabled by default from 22:00 to 07:00 local computer time. Disable it independently or adjust either time in 15-minute steps. Equal times mean no restriction. Settings remain accessible from the main menu.
[*]Exit Game and Esc remain available during breaks. The native exit confirmation appears above the break panel. Unlocking after a night restriction never automatically ends a turn.
[/list]

[h2]Break saves[/h2]
Each break attempts a separate local normal save named RestGuard_Idle/Time/Turn/Night plus the turn, timestamp, and unique suffix. These do not overwrite default autosaves.
Keep the newest 10 by default, adjustable to 1–100. After a new save is confirmed, older RestGuard saves are removed. The limit covers all break types and campaigns. Other manual saves, autosaves, quicksaves, cloud saves, and other mods' saves are untouched. Failed cleanup stops safely and can try again after the next successful break save. Wait for the panel to confirm saving before exiting.

[h2]Installation and validation[/h2]
Subscribe, wait for Steam to download, restart, and enable No More Turn in Additional Content. Avoid duplicate manual and Workshop copies. Single-player only; no dependency on another break or save mod. Compatible with the before-turn save hook in Auto-save Before....
1.8.5 passed 209 offline checks, including English/Chinese text, night-time boundaries, retention, and before-turn save integration. This release was not tested in a running game.

设置入口：选项 → 附加内容 → No More Turn（不再来一回合）。回合、时间、夜间限制可分别开关。
[list]
[*]回合休息：在会话内累计结束回合次数，读档保留但不增加，退出清零。每20回合一档，默认间隔5／4／3／2／1回合，可调整。

[*]主动休息：空闲1分钟显示面板，随时可继续；满5分钟共同重置回合与时间计数，提前继续保留累计。
[*]夜间禁玩：默认开启22:00–次日07:00，采用电脑本地时间。可单独关闭，起止按15分钟调整；起止相同不限制。锁定期间仍可退出，到主菜单修改设置。
[*]休息存档：每次休息另存独立RestGuard文件，默认保留最近10个，可设1–100个。新存档确认成功后才清理旧文件，跨战役合计，不动原版自动、快速、其他手动、云端及其他Mod存档。
[*]强制休息期间“退出游戏”和Esc始终可用，原生确认框显示在上层。退出前请确认保存成功。
[/list]
