# Fenhe — Division and Unification — Civ VII Rescue Project

What if an age change followed a period of political division and unification, rather than simply swapping civilizations? Fenhe explores that idea: simulate changes from the existing world, choose a snapshot, then continue as a new power. It is currently an experimental prototype requiring a local helper, not a plug-and-play Workshop mod.

## 1. Explore another political map

A familiar map usually comes with familiar borders. Imagining a different political history often means starting from scratch.

Fenhe uses the current world’s settlements and powers as the starting point for division and unification. City locations provide continuity while political ownership can change.

## 2. Choose the moment you want to play

The most interesting simulated state may not be the last one: perhaps many rivals have emerged, or a few large powers are taking shape.

Choose a more fragmented or unified snapshot from the simulation as the basis of the next scenario, rather than always accepting a single final outcome.

## 3. Continue as a power in the new world

Watching the result is one thing; taking control of one of those powers is the more interesting possibility.

Choose one human-controlled power and build the new-age scenario with the corresponding city ownership, major powers and city-states. Selected samples have completed entry and save/reload checks, but remain limited prototype results.

## Availability and testing

The 0.2.0-alpha prototype combines game and UI modules and requires local helper/debug setup. Specific Windows and Mac 1.5.0 scenarios have records of generation, entry and save loading, not universal map or age coverage. It is not a complete native age-transition replacement and does not preserve population, great works, history, technology or entire armies losslessly.

For now, this is a development preview. I would be interested in the situations where you would find it most useful.
